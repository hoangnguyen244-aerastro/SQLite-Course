TEMA 1 - BAI 1 (b1) - HUONG DAN CHAY TREN WINDOWS

1. Dat bo CodeBlocks2 cua giang vien vao C:\Prog\CodeBlocks2.
2. Tao thu muc C:\Work\b1.
3. Chep 2 file main.c va b1.cbp vao C:\Work\b1.
4. Mo C:\Prog\CodeBlocks2\codeblocks.exe.
5. File > Open > C:\Work\b1\b1.cbp.
6. Settings > Editor > Encoding: UTF-8.
7. Nhan F9 (Build and run), sau do nhan Information.
8. Neu hop thoai hien thi phien ban SQLite, chup anh man hinh va chen vao bao cao PDF.

Khac voi mau GitHub: b1.cbp da bo sung duong dan header va static import library SQLite;
main.c thay sqlite3_libversion() lam chuoi dinh dang bang "%s" de an toan hon.

Chua chay kiem thu tren may Windows cua ban. Neu co loi compiler/linker,
chup man hinh Build log de kiem tra.
